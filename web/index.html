Every calendar. One link.

Connect your Google calendars, paste any .ics link, and ubercal keeps them merged into share links that refresh every ten minutes. Each link decides, calendar by calendar, what it shows: everything, only busy time, or nothing.

01 how it works

In from everywhere. Out as one calendar, as many times as you need.

01

connect

Sign in with Google and pick the calendars to merge, from as many Google accounts as you use. Paste any .ics or webcal link too: a team rota, a booking system, a holiday calendar.

02

choose

Make a share link for each audience. In every link, each calendar is shown in full, as busy time with nothing else, or not at all.

full busy off
03

subscribe

Add the link to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook. ubercal reads your calendars every ten minutes; how often your calendar app asks for the link is up to the app.

02 what it keeps

Only what it needs to merge, and nothing it does not.

  • read-onlyubercal asks Google to read your calendars, never to change them.
  • sealedGoogle's access tokens and your private .ics links are encrypted at rest.
  • busy means busyA calendar shown as busy leaves ubercal with its times only: no titles, places, people, notes or alarms.
  • secret linksEvery share link is a long random address. Make a new one and the old one stops working at once.
  • gone means goneDelete your account and your calendars, events and links are deleted with it. Full details in the privacy policy.
